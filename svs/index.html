SVS: Stable View Synthesis from Mobile Multi-Camera Rigs via Geometry-Guided Diffusion Model

📝 Abstract

Synthesizing a stable, fixed viewpoint from a mobile multi-camera system is essential for spatial intelligence and scene understanding. However, this task remains challenging because mobile multi-camera rigs usually capture only a few synchronized viewpoints at each time step, which is insufficient for dense reconstruction methods. At the same time, the inherent motion of the camera rig introduces viewpoint variations across time, making generative models prone to temporal inconsistencies including flickering and geometric distortions. Existing approaches struggle to balance geometric accuracy with photorealistic synthesis while maintaining stability across time. In this paper, we present a framework that addresses these challenges through geometry-guided latent diffusion. The proposed method aligns dynamic multi-camera observations into a temporally unified spatial representation, then a multi-view geometric estimator produces coarse structural bases from the target viewpoint, which are utilized as geometric priors for a conditional diffusion model. By training on paired sequences that associate these structural bases with high-fidelity observations, the model learns to synthesize temporally consistent, high-quality videos while preserving scene geometry. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our method achieves improved temporal coherence and visual fidelity compared with other approaches, providing an effective solution for stationary view synthesis from mobile multi-camera systems. 📖 Method Overview

Our framework addresses the challenges of stable view synthesis from mobile multi-camera rigs through geometry-guided latent diffusion. The method aligns dynamic multi-camera observations into a temporally unified spatial representation, leveraging geometric priors to guide the diffusion process for temporally consistent synthesis.


🎯 Key Contributions

1 We develop a framework that bridges sparse geometric priors and generative models through spatial frame alignment and geometry-conditioned latent diffusion, facilitating stable view synthesis from moving multi-camera rigs.

2 We propose a paired training strategy that explicitly associates coarse structural bases with high-fidelity observations, allowing the model to learn visual sequence mapping while maintaining temporal consistency.

3 Through extensive experiments across diverse scenarios, we demonstrate that our method achieves improved temporal coherence and visual fidelity compared to other approaches, and provides an effective solution for observer view synthesis from mobile multi-camera systems.
